Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How quickly can you start on my project? +

We typically begin new projects within 1-2 weeks of signing. During this time, we'll conduct our discovery process to understand your business and goals, then develop a customized strategy before implementation begins.

Do you offer free consultations? +

Yes! We offer a free initial consultation where we'll discuss your business, current marketing efforts, and goals. We'll provide insights and recommendations, with no obligation to proceed. Fill out our contact form or call us to schedule.

What is your minimum contract length? +

We don't believe in locking clients into long-term contracts. Our services are month-to-month because we want to earn your business every single month. Some services, like SEO, work best with at least 6 months of commitment to see optimal results.

How do you report on results? +

We provide comprehensive monthly reports that include all relevant metrics, insights, and recommendations. You'll also have access to a client dashboard for real-time data. We schedule monthly review calls to discuss progress and strategy.

Do you work with businesses outside the Gulf Coast? +

While our primary focus is the Gulf Coast region of Alabama, we do work with clients throughout the Southeast and nationwide. Our local expertise gives us a strong foundation, but our digital marketing skills are applicable to businesses anywhere.
